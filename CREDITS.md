# Credits

Third-party assets bundled with The Frontier.

## 3D models

- **Space Kit** by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit) — CC0 1.0 (public domain).
  - `public/models/*.glb`: hangars, structures, machines, satellite dishes, rocket parts, platforms, rocks, craters, and crystals.
  - `src/assets/models/`: the original module, rover, and astronaut set.

## Textures

  - `public/textures/moon_regolith_*.jpg`
- **Blue Marble** Earth imagery from [NASA Visible Earth](https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/collection/1484/blue-marble) — public domain (NASA imagery is not copyrighted; credit NASA).
  - `public/textures/earth_blue_marble.jpg`
